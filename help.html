How to actually buy something from 1688?

That is a very good question and here is the answer: 1688 only sells to mainland China and not the outside world, so you have to get creative and that is why Agents exist.

  • Step 1: Create an account with an Agent (I recommend Superbuy because they are the oldest agent in the game (13 years), they have the best shipping, no fees and incredible customer service)
  • Step 2: Paste the link of the 1688 item you want to buy in the agent search bar
  • Step 3: Buy the item and wait for it to arrive to the agent warehouse and they will send you multiple photos of the product
  • Step 4: After it arrives to the warehouse, you can then send your item or your multiple items to your doorstep.
