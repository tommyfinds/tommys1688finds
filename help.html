Help

Everything you need to know about how Tommy's 1688 Finds works.

What is this site?

Tommy's 1688 Finds is a curated list of cheap, non-branded products found on 1688.com — a Chinese wholesale marketplace. Every listing here links straight to the original product page on 1688. We don't sell anything ourselves, and we don't hold or ship stock.

How do I actually buy something?

1688 sells to buyers inside mainland China, so most people outside China use a shipping/purchasing agent:

  • Create an account with an agent (e.g. Wegobuy, CSSBuy, Superbuy, Basetao)
  • Paste the 1688 product link into your agent account
  • The agent buys the item on your behalf and receives it at their warehouse
  • Once it arrives, they ship it internationally to your address

Delivery usually takes 7–20 days depending on the shipping line you choose.

Why use an agent instead of buying directly?

1688 is built for businesses buying in mainland China and doesn't offer international checkout or shipping. An agent acts as your local buyer and forwarder — they handle the Chinese-language checkout, payment, and customs paperwork for international shipping.

Are prices shown here final?

No — prices on this site reflect the product's listed price on 1688 only. They don't include your agent's service fee, domestic shipping to the warehouse, international shipping, or any customs duties that may apply in your country.

Can I get quality check (QC) photos before it ships?

Most agents offer this as an optional service — once your item arrives at their warehouse, you can request photos before it's shipped further. Check your chosen agent's site for how to request this.
